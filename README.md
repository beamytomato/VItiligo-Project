# Corresponding Landmark Labeler App

The purpose of this app is to help make skin imaging more consistent between visits by accurately guiding the metal imaging ring placed on the same skin location. It compares a baseline image and a follow-up image, uses visible skin landmarks such as moles, spots, or marks to align the two photos, and then measures the displacement between the original ring center and the new ring center in millimeters. This is important because even a small shift in ring placement could mean the camera is imaging a slightly different skin area, which could affect whether treatment changes are measured accurately. Overall, the app helps provide visual and quantitative evidence that the same anatomical skin site was captured across imaging sessions. Dr. Mihaela Balu at the Beckman Laser Institute at UC Irvine was having challenges with placing the metal ring in the exact same location between visits. The ring is important because it attaches to her FLAME microscope which provides images of the affected skin cells undergoing treatment.
